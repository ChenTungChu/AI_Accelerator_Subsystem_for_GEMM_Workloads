C1.3 governing rule
===================
C1.3 is NOT another exclusion campaign and is NOT a percentage-farming phase.

Only legal, meaningful, bug-finding-oriented constructive coverage is in scope.
Improvement is measured from fresh RAW coverage, not from additional exclusions.

No force/deposit/state corruption.
No broad line-wide exclusions.
No reclassification of repaired bugs as waivers.
No reopening stable IP merely to improve project-level percentage.
No global toggle-percentage target.

C1.3 success criteria
=====================
C1.3 does not have a required aggregate percentage.

Success means:
  - every selected P0 object is either constructively hit by legal stimulus or
    explicitly rejected from the target set after exact reachability analysis;
  - no functional regression is introduced;
  - any discovered real bug is repaired and regression-tested, never excluded;
  - fresh RAW coverage delta is reported separately from effective coverage;
  - toggle changes are informational only;
  - C1.2 exclusions and protected residues remain intact.
