Priority policy
===============
P0:
  AXI/protocol/error/recovery paths with high bug-finding value.

P1:
  Exact-residue audit required before any new stimulus.

P2:
  Stable-IP, configuration-disabled, already-dispositioned, or percentage-only
  targets that are explicitly not worth pursuing in this milestone.

Hard constraints
================
- legal stimulus only
- no force/deposit
- no state corruption
- no global toggle target
- no broad exclusion campaign
- repaired bugs are never waivers
- RAW delta is the improvement metric
- effective coverage remains an audit view
- preserve the existing UVM architecture and canonical smoke tests
- prefer extending existing tests over adding standalone tests
- preserve regression count unless a genuinely distinct test is necessary

C1.3-A closure condition
